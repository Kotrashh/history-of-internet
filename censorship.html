Freedom & Censorship

From a free and open internet in the 1990s, to bans on YouTube, Twitter, and Wikipedia.

Until 2007, Turkey's internet was largely unrestricted. Then Law No. 5651 changed everything. Growing up in Turkey, I experienced these bans personally — I remember using VPNs as a teenager to access YouTube, and watching the Twitter ban announced just before local elections in 2014. By 2023, over 500,000 websites had been blocked under this law.

🔒 VPN Use in Turkey

Due to repeated platform bans, Turkey consistently ranks among the highest VPN-using countries in Europe. When Twitter was banned in 2014, citizens wrote the Google DNS address 8.8.8.8 on walls around Istanbul to share the bypass with others.
