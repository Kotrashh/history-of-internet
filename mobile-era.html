The Mobile Era

From 3G in 2009 to 45.7 million 4.5G subscribers in 2016 - how smartphones changed everything.

The mobile internet era was the most transformative period in Turkey's digital history. During my teenage years, smartphones went from luxury items to everyday essentials. Turkey's 4.5G launch in April 2016 was a landmark moment - suddenly the internet felt genuinely fast everywhere. Turkey quickly ranked among the world's top countries for mobile internet adoption, reaching over 68 million users by 2022.
