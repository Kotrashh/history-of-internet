About This Project

I was born in Turkey in 2001, the same year ADSL broadband was first introduced by TTNET. Growing up, I witnessed the country's internet landscape transform dramatically - from expensive dial-up connections and internet cafes, to high-speed 4.5G mobile internet in everyone's pocket, and the complex political story of social media bans and censorship laws. This website explores those three chapters of Turkey's internet history.
