The Early Internet in Turkey

From Turkey's first connection in 1993 to ADSL broadband in 2001 - the year I was born.

Turkey's internet journey began in academic institutions long before it reached homes and businesses. The period from 1993 to 2008 laid the technical and commercial foundations for everything that followed. METU (Middle East Technical University) in Ankara played a crucial role - their 64 kbps connection to the NSFNet on 12 April 1993 marked Turkey's entry into the global internet.

🏛️ The Role of METU

Middle East Technical University established Turkey's first internet connection in 1993. For years, universities remained the primary access points before commercial ISPs emerged and eventually TTNET launched ADSL broadband in 2001.
